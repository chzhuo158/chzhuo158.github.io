Apple Inc. · Hardware Technology (HWT) · ATG

12/2022 – Present

Senior RTL Design Engineer

  • Responsible for high-speed serial RXA and TXA modeling, RTL design, and verification at advanced TSMC nodes.
  • Developed robust RX and TX models for high-speed SERDES circuits, facilitating fast and accurate mixed-signal simulations for large-scale architectures.
  • Enabled early identification of potential design issues and provided critical feedback to circuit and digital RTL designers for optimization.
  • Extensively involved in verification of CTLE, VGA, sampler, DFE, DCO, TXA driver models, CDR, EQ engine, and offset cancellation.
  • AI & Workflow Innovation. Developed numerous SKILL scripts to accelerate model development, verification, and sign-off, while leveraging AI for automated design documentation.

University of Michigan

09/2017 – 11/2022

Graduate Student Research Assistant

Advisor: Prof. Zhong He

  • Developed a back-end DSP ASIC (TSMC 65nm PDK) for 100MHz data acquisition and waveform processing of pixelated CdZnTe radiation sensors in a 7x7mm 64-pin QFN package.
  • Built test systems and firmware based on Xilinx Zynq-7 FPGA, paired with a Qt Framework acquisition software.
  • Verified and debugged noise performance of analog front-end signal readout ASICs (TSMC 130nm) across process corners.
  • Led research on sensor detection efficiency (60keV to 2.6MeV gamma rays) and identified efficiency loss mechanisms using customized Geant4 particle transport simulations.

Projects

Built from the gate up.

Class and research projects across digital VLSI and analog IC design.

EECS470 · 09/2020 – 12/2020

Super-scalar Out-of-Order 32-bit RISC-V Microprocessor

Designed and synthesized a two-way super-scalar, out-of-order RISC-V microprocessor in SystemVerilog. Synthesized with Intel 130nm PDK running at 100MHz in simulation. Implemented instruction prefetching, g-share branch predictor, load-store queue, write-back data cache, and automated validation script systems.

SystemVerilog · RISC-V · Intel 130nm

EECS427 · 09/2019 – 12/2019

16-bit Microprocessor with CORDIC Coprocessor

Designed and optimized timing performance of standard logic gates at the physical layout level. Laid out a 16-bit microprocessor using Virtuoso and Design Compiler (Intel 130nm PDK), extending functionality to compute trigonometric functions via the CORDIC algorithm.

Cadence Virtuoso · CORDIC · Layout Design

NERS590 · 09/2019 – 12/2019

Ultra-low-noise Charge Amplifier for Radiation Sensors

Designed an ultra-low-noise charge amplifier for semiconductor radiation sensors under advisor Gianluigi De Geronimo. Optimized noise performance extensively by tuning shaping times and transistor sizing.

Analog Design · Low-Noise · SPICE

NERS522 · 01/2019 – 04/2019

Advanced Analog Integrated Circuits

Designed RF Low-Noise Amplifiers (LNA), Mixers, and Voltage-Controlled Oscillators (VCO) as part of advanced RF/Analog integrated circuit coursework.

RF LNA · Mixer & VCO · Cadence
